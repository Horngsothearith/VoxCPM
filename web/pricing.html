Simple, pay-as-you-go pricing

Buy a credit pack once, use it whenever. One credit covers one character of speech, across voice design, cloning and streaming.

Online payments aren't enabled on this server yet. Contact the administrator to add credits to your account.

Questions

How are credits counted?

Each request costs 1 credit per character of input text (the text to speak, not the voice description). Credits are charged when generation starts and refunded automatically if it fails.

Do credits expire?

No. Credit packs are one-time purchases and stay on your account until used.

Can I use credits through the API?

Yes. The web playground and every API key on your account draw from the same balance.

Is there a free tier?

Every new account starts with free credits, no card required.
